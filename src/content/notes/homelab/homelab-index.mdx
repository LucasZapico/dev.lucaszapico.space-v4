## Monitoring

### Uptime Kuma

*   **Link:** [uptimeKuma]()
*   **Description:** Uptime Kuma is a self-hosted status monitoring solution that allows you to monitor the uptime of your services. It provides a web interface to track the status of your services, send notifications when they go down, and visualize their uptime history. It's a great alternative to commercial services like UptimeRobot.

### ntfy

*   **Link:** [https://ntfy.sh/](https://ntfy.sh/)
*   **Description:**  ntfy is a simple, self-hosted notification service. It lets you send push notifications to any device (Android, iOS, desktop) using a unique topic name. It's great for home automation, server monitoring, or any situation where you need a reliable way to receive alerts. No account needed! You simply publish to a topic and subscribe to it on your devices.

### beszel

*   **Link:** [https://beszel.io/](https://beszel.io/)
*   **Description:** Beszel is a self-hosted alternative to Twitter/X. It's a federated, minimalist microblogging platform built with Go. It emphasizes simplicity, privacy, and user control. You can host your own instance and connect with other Beszel instances through the ActivityPub protocol (the same protocol used by Mastodon).
